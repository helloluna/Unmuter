## 1. PTA / SBS – Case Documents

These files show how SBS approved and maintained the harness over three years, and how PTA responded when asked to review and step down its use.

### 1.1 Approval Letters (2022)

- 📄 [Orion_SBS_Approval_Letter.PDF](/resources/Orion_SBS_Approval_Letter.PDF)
- 📄 [Linden_SBS_Approval_Letter.PDF](/resources/Linden_SBS_Approval_Letter.PDF)

> Transport assistance approval letters confirming school bus access and the use of a harness, without any attached clinical assessment or fade-out plan.  
> **Orion** and **Linden** are pseudonyms used on this site to protect the children’s identities.

### 1.2 SBS Email Chains (2022–2025)

- 📄 [SBS_email_chain_2022.pdf](/resources/SBS_email_chain_2022.pdf)
- 📄 [SBS_email_chain_2025.pdf](/resources/SBS_email_chain_2025.pdf)

> Email correspondence between the family, school and SBS.  
> Shows how harness use was introduced and then left in place for three years with no review, and how requests for documentation and a step-down plan were redirected back to the family.

### 1.3 PTA “Final Response” (2025)

- 📄 [PTS_Ombusdman_letter.pdf](/resources/PTS_Ombusdman_letter.pdf)
- 📄 [sbs_statement_cover_letter.pdf](/resources/sbs_statement_cover_letter.pdf)

> PTA’s written position in response to the Ombudsman referral.  
> Admits there was no review of the harness, and states that “families are responsible” for initiating professional assessment.

### 1.4 SBS Policies

- 📄 [SBS_POLICY_2020.pdf](/resources/SBS_POLICY_2020.pdf)
- 📄 [SBS_POLICY_2025.pdf](/resources/SBS_POLICY_2025.pdf)

> Student Transport Assistance Policy (STAP) documents.  
> The later policy introduces a requirement for a “qualified prescriber” and review schedule, highlighting the earlier policy gap.

## 3. Equal Opportunity Commission WA (EOC)

These files document the state discrimination complaint and the limits of EOC’s jurisdiction.

- 📄 [EOC_FIRST_ASSESSMENT.pdf](/resources/EOC_FIRST_ASSESSMENT.pdf)
- 📄 [EOC_ACCEPTANCE.pdf](/resources/EOC_ACCEPTANCE.pdf)
- 📄 [Eoc_email_chain.pdf](/resources/Eoc_email_chain.pdf)

> The EOC correspondence confirms that the matter is accepted under the Equal Opportunity Act 1984 (WA) as an impairment discrimination complaint, but that EOC cannot deal with federal disability standards or restrictive-practices frameworks.

### “Good Cause” (Out-of-time Allegations)

**Process:** Request → Submission → Decision

- 📄 Request: [EOC_Good_Cause.pdf](/resources/EOC_Good_Cause.pdf)
- 📄 Submission: [Good_Cause_Submission.pdf](/resources/Good_Cause_Submission.pdf)
- 📄 Decision: [Good_Cause_not_Accepted.pdf](/resources/Good_Cause_not_Accepted.pdf)

> This process effectively determines which years of conduct can be formally examined, and which years become “background only”.

## 4. Australian Human Rights Commission (AHRC)

These materials show the attempt to bring the case under the Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Cth), and how section 13(4) was used to refuse the complaint.

- 📄 [AHRC_Complaint_form.pdf](/resources/AHRC_Complaint_form.pdf)
- 📄 [Authority_to_Act.pdf](/resources/Authority_to_Act.pdf)
- 📄 [ahrc_case_closure.pdf](/resources/ahrc_case_closure.pdf)
- 📄 [Ahrc_email_chain.pdf](/resources/Ahrc_email_chain.pdf)

> The complaint was lodged under the DDA with authority to act on behalf of non-verbal children.  
> The Commission then closed the matter under section 13(4) because a related complaint was already on foot at the state level (EOC WA), and later confirmed that this bar would remain in place.
